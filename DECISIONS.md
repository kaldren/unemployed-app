- 2026-10-27 — Engaging UI: palette moves from coral to electric violet (`#6D28D9`) plus a violet → pink → amber "spark" gradient (`Button variant="spark"`, logo, progress, match moment). Candidate home adds a greeting, today's progress (reviewed vs. left in the deck, so the goal is always finite) and a daily streak (days in a row with a swipe, computed from `candidate_swipes.created_at` in **UTC**, no migration; a streak ending yesterday still shows until today ends). Cards show "N% skills match". A mutual match gets a confetti celebration instead of an alert. Ethics rules (no guilt, no fake scarcity, no infinite feed) are part of the design system. `DESIGN.md` is renamed `DESIGNSYSTEM.md`, the one design doc every agent follows.
- 2026-10-26 — Local database: `pnpm dev` and `pnpm test:e2e` run against a local Supabase stack (Supabase CLI on OrbStack's Docker engine; `supabase/config.toml`), not the hosted project, unless `pnpm db:use remote` (stored in git-ignored `.db-target`). Full Supabase rather than plain Postgres, because the app needs Auth and Storage. Ports are 553xx (not the CLI's 543xx default) so it runs next to other local Supabase projects. `scripts/db.mts` starts OrbStack and the stack when needed, applies the Drizzle migrations and writes `.env.development.local`, which Next and every script (`scripts/load-env.mts`) read before `.env.local`. Locally: verify and sign-in rate limits are raised (the hosted 30-burst limit failed e2e runs), email via Resend is off, a seeded admin (`admin@seed.test`, fixed id) is in `ADMIN_USER_IDS`, edge functions and analytics are off. Anthropic and other keys still come from `.env.local`. Google/LinkedIn OAuth aren't configured locally.
- 2026-10-26 — Dev seed: `pnpm dev` first runs `scripts/seed.mts`, which deletes every account and company under `*.seed.test` and recreates 6 candidates, 4 companies (3 approved, 1 needs review) with 6 employers and 10 jobs (open, draft, closed), pre-scored `job_matches` (model `seed`), one mutual match and one pending like; then it prints and writes `.seed-accounts.md` (git-ignored) with a one-time, 1-hour sign-in link per account. Deterministic data, so tests and screenshots are repeatable. When dev points at the hosted project (`pnpm db:use remote`), seed rows stay isolated: matching never pairs a seed company with a real candidate or the reverse, and email to seed addresses is skipped; `matched_at` is set to 9999-01-01 so viewing a seeded deck never starts a paid AI run (editing a seeded profile or job still rescores it); the seeded needs-review company shows in `/admin` only in development; seed skills and roles missing from the shared catalog are added and kept. A seed failure doesn't block `pnpm dev`; `pnpm seed` re-seeds alone (and fails loudly). Refuses to run on Vercel or with `NODE_ENV=production`.
- 2026-10-26 — Email: Resend's REST API through `fetch` (no SDK, no React Email yet: two plain templates with inline styles). Sending is skipped, with a log line, until `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` are set. "It's a match" goes to the candidate and the employer who liked, in `after()` from whichever swipe completes the match. A daily digest (Vercel Cron, `vercel.json`, 07:00 UTC → `/api/cron/digest`, guarded by `CRON_SECRET`) emails each candidate how many unswiped deck jobs the AI first scored (`job_matches.first_scored_at`, so rescoring doesn't make old jobs "new") since the last run checked them; none new → no email, but they're stamped anyway so the 500-per-run batch moves on. A repeated like doesn't send the match email again. Candidates can turn the digest off in Profile → Account (match emails always go). No digest for employers yet. Supabase's sign-in emails still use its built-in SMTP until Resend SMTP is set in the Supabase dashboard.
- 2026-10-26 — Landing and legal: `/` shows a landing page to visitors (pitch, "Find a job" / "Hire with us") and the deck to signed-in candidates; the proxy makes `/` (exactly), `/privacy` and `/terms` public. Privacy policy and terms are a plain-language GDPR-style draft naming `NEXT_PUBLIC_OPERATOR_NAME` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL`; they need a legal review before launch. Only the essential sign-in cookie is used, so there's no cookie banner. Sign-in pages say "By continuing you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy" (no checkbox). Minimum age 16.
- 2026-10-26 — Account deletion: "Delete my account" (with `confirm()`) is in a new "Account" profile tab for candidates and at the bottom of the employer home. It deletes the `auth.users` row (everything cascades) plus the candidate's resume files (new Storage delete policy, through their own session). An employer's company is deleted with them only when no members remain; when the owner leaves, the oldest teammate becomes owner. Data export is by email request for now (the privacy policy says so).
- 2026-10-26 — Job location and pay: jobs get country + city and a yearly gross salary range + currency. Publishing needs the salary range (EU pay transparency, and candidates' top question) and, for on-site/hybrid jobs, a country; drafts need neither. Remote with no country means "anywhere". New hard filters (SQL, before and after scoring): the candidate lives in the job's country, or is open to relocation for an on-site/hybrid job; and the job's max salary reaches the candidate's minimum when both use the same currency (no FX conversion; different currencies don't filter). Job cards show "Sofia, Bulgaria · EUR 50,000–70,000 a year". The AI prompt gets the job's location only when set, so existing jobs keep their input hash and aren't rescored. The AI drafter doesn't fill pay or location (it must not invent them).
- 2026-10-26 — Company vetting: a new company is vetted in `after()` (no Inngest yet). The server fetches `https://<domain>/` (8s timeout, 500 KB, up to 3 redirects, each host re-checked: no IPs, ports or internal names), turns it into text and Claude (`VETTING_MODEL`, default Haiku 5.5) answers approve / reject / needs_review with a reason. Approve → `approved`, so jobs go live with no human step; anything else waits for `/admin`. A failed or unconfigured AI call becomes `needs_review`, never a silent approve. A run first claims the company by stamping `vetted_at`, so concurrent page views start one run; the result is written only while the company is still `pending`, so an admin's choice always wins. Besides the hostname check, every address the domain resolves to must be public (DNS rebinding between the check and the fetch remains possible, but the page text only reaches Claude). The model is recorded only when it actually answered. Reserved domains (example.com, *.test) are never vetted (e2e uses example.com). The employer home re-triggers vetting for pending companies, which backfills existing ones. `/admin` (ids in `ADMIN_USER_IDS`) lists every non-approved company with the AI's reason; non-admins get a 404. Employers see "We're verifying your company" or a "couldn't verify" note with `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL`.
- 2026-10-26 — e2e runs its own dev server on :3100 with `NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-e2e`, so it never reuses your :3000 server, and Playwright can pass it test-only env (the e2e admin's id in `ADMIN_USER_IDS`).
- 2026-10-26 — Match gate is skill coverage, not the overall score: a pair reaches both decks when the candidate has **more than 75%** of the job's skills. Claude lists which job skills the profile covers, judging skills, experience and about, so equivalents count ("Postgres" for "PostgreSQL"); the code keeps only real job skill names and computes the share (`job_matches.matched_skills`, `skill_coverage`). The 0–100 score is still stored and orders the decks. Work mode and employment type stay hard filters. Unswiped rows scored before this have coverage 0 and are hidden until rescored (the prompt changed, so every pair is rescored on its next run); a pair either side already swiped is never rescored, so the migration keeps its old verdict (score ≥ 70 → coverage 100).
- 2026-10-25 — AI matching v1: decks show only pairs Claude scored ≥ 70/100 (`job_matches`, with both reasons, model and an input hash, so every score is auditable and an unchanged pair is never re-scored). Scoring model `MATCH_MODEL`, default `claude-sonnet-5-5` (per TECH.md), effort medium. The AI never sees names, emails or links. Pipeline: SQL hard filters (live job, complete visible candidate, job's work mode in the candidate's, employment type if the candidate picked any, neither side swiped yet) → top 25 pairs by shared skills → one Claude call each, 4 at a time. Runs in `after()` when a published job is saved or a profile section is saved, and on a deck view when the last run is over 24h old (that also backfills existing data). **No Inngest or Voyage yet**: they'd need accounts and keys; shared-skill ranking stands in for embeddings, and the 24h refresh for the nightly cron. Hard filters also apply at read time, so a stale score can't show a pair that no longer fits. A swiped pair keeps its score. Each side sees its own "Why it fits" reason on the card. Jobs have no location field yet, so location isn't filtered. No refusal fallback: a refused or failed pair stays unscored and is retried on the next run; a run where every call fails is retried after an hour, not a day. Each run scores unscored pairs first, then the longest since scored.

- 2026-10-24 — Matching v1 (swipes): candidates swipe published jobs from verified companies on `/`; employers swipe candidates per published job at `/employers/jobs/[id]/candidates`. A like from both sides is a match, computed from `candidate_swipes` + `employer_swipes` (no `matches` table yet; the AI-scored nightly matching will add one). Candidates who already liked a job are shown to its employer first. Employers see the profile without name, email or links until a match. Contact after a match is email (mailto) plus LinkedIn; Realtime chat and "It's a match" emails come later. Swipes are final in the UI (no undo). Only complete profiles appear in decks, and `hidden_company_domains` hides a candidate both ways. Unverified companies can't review candidates.
- 2026-10-24 — Job status: a "Status" select (Draft / Published) in the job editor, saved with Save. "Published" is the existing `open` enum value (no migration). Publishing also needs a description and at least one skill; drafts don't. If the company isn't approved yet, the hint says candidates will see it once the company is verified.
- 2026-10-24 — AI drafts also pick seniority / employment type / work mode: on a new post it fills only what the employer left blank (their picks stay); on refine it keeps them unless the instruction changes them. All three are required to save a post (DB columns stay nullable; "Write it myself" may still start with blanks).
- 2026-10-24 — Job drafting model comes from `JOB_DRAFT_MODEL` (server env only, no UI), defaulting to `claude-haiku-5-5`.
- 2026-10-24 — Job posts v1: employer picks seniority / employment type / work mode, writes a brief; Claude Haiku 5.5 drafts title, description (plain text) and skills. "Refine with AI" rewrites what's on screen without saving. "Write it myself" makes an empty draft. All jobs stay `draft` (no publish yet). Skills go through the shared `skills` catalog via `job_skills` (ordered by `position`), ready for matching. AI controls are hidden/disabled when `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is unset.
